    How To Play

    1. Read the question carefully before you start the tape. Different tapes will feature different question types, but all are about something in common across all the tapes.
    2. Questions will always be specific enough that you can know the answer from just one song.
    3. A tape contains clips from six songs, 10-second each, back to back.
    4. Lyrics containing the title of a song will never be part of the clip.
    5. You can guess as many times as you like.
    6. Guess the correct answer and the full tracklist will be revealed. You can also copy your result to share with friends!
