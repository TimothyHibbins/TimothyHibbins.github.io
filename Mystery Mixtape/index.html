    How To Play

    Welcome to Mystery Mixtape!

    In this daily musical quiz game, you listen to a special mixtape containing 10-second clips from six different songs, ordered from least known to most famous. Your goal is to answer a question about what they have in common from listening to as little of the tape as possible with as few guesses as possible—but there is no time limit or guess limit.

    The commonality is usually in the songs' titles, either a shared word or related words, but it may sometimes be something else about the songs. This will always be specified in the question, so read it carefully before you start the tape.

    Guess the correct answer (or give up) and a table with the full list of songs will be revealed.

    A few tips to keep in mind:

    1. You can pause and unpause by clicking/tapping the tape.
    2. Answers will always be gettable from any one song.
    3. A song's 10-second clip will never contain its title in the lyrics you hear.
    4. Answers are case-insensitive.
    5. Alternate ways of writing the answer may be accepted (e.g. LA will be accepted for Los Angeles).

    A new mystery mixtape releases every midnight, Melbourne time (AEST). You can also play through the entire archive of past mixtapes at your leisure.

    Have fun, and good luck!
